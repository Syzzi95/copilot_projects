# Supply Chain Compliance Review Project

## Overview

This project leverages Microsoft Copilot across Outlook, Teams, Word, Excel, and PowerPoint to identify, analyze, and address critical supply chain issues affecting FDA compliance. The workflow covers issue identification, stakeholder coordination, risk analysis, mitigation planning, and executive reporting.

# Project task and Descriptions

## Task 1: Problem Identification and Action Prioritization

**Description:**  
Analyze a supply chain compliance email to identify key business challenges, prioritize issues, and define appropriate corrective actions.

**Expected Deliverables:**
- Email summary highlighting key findings
- Identification of at least three critical supply chain issues
- Five actionable next steps
- **Supply Chain Issues Review** report including:
  - Introduction
  - Outline of the Business Problem
  - Action Items
  - Conclusion

---

## Task 2: Cross-Functional Meeting Coordination and Action Planning

**Description:**  
Coordinate stakeholder communication, document key discussion points from a cross-functional meeting, and develop a structured action plan.

**Expected Deliverables:**
- Meeting invitation titled **"Supply Chain Issues Review"**
- Meeting agenda and stakeholder list
- Summary of the Teams meeting transcript
- **Action Plan** document including:
  - General Strategies
  - Immediate Actions
  - Short-Term Actions (1–3 Weeks)
  - Ongoing Actions

---

## Task 3: Supply Chain Risk Analysis and Mitigation

**Description:**  
Analyze supply chain performance data to evaluate risks, supplier performance, delivery timelines, and compliance impacts.

**Expected Deliverables:**
- Excel analysis covering:
  - Average delivery timeline by risk level
  - On-time delivery rate for delayed components (CARB123, BIND567, COAT890)
  - Supplier performance breakdown
- **Supply Chain Issue Analysis Report** including:
  - Executive Summary
  - Component Overview
  - Implications for Compliance
  - Root Cause Analysis
  - General Mitigation Strategies
  - Component-Specific Mitigation Strategies

---

## Task 4: Stakeholder Report Creation and Presentation

**Description:**  
Create an executive-level presentation summarizing supply chain risks, compliance implications, and recommended mitigation actions.

**Expected Deliverables:**
- **Supply Chain Issue Compliance Report** presentation
- Professional and consistently formatted slide deck
- Content aligned with findings from the analysis report and Excel data
- Speaker notes for all presentation sections
